## Engineering Problem

A building structure must transfer dead, imposed, wind and other applicable loads safely from slabs through beams and columns into the foundation and supporting soil.

The design must achieve:

- strength;
- stability;
- serviceability;
- durability;
- reasonable constructability;
- economical material use;
- clear detailing.
